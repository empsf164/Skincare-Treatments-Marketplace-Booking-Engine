Patient Safety Governance

The 5-Step LUMÉA Verification Standard

Fewer than 18% of aesthetic clinics meet our strict credentialing requirements. Here is how we safeguard your treatment quality.

1

Board License Audits

Every physician, dermatologist, and aesthetician is verified directly against government medical council registers with zero license lapses allowed.

2

Device Serial Matching

We inspect serial numbers for all laser, RF, and hydro-infusion systems with original OEMs (Hydrafacial, Cynosure, InMode) to prevent counterfeit machines.

3

Sterile Hygiene Logs

Surprise clinical site inspections evaluate autoclave spore testing, single-use consumable packaging, and medical disposal compliance.

4

Verified Patient Reviews

Only patients who paid deposits and completed verified appointments through LUMÉA can publish clinic ratings and recovery feedback.
