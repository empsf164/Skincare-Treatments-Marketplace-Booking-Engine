Frequently Asked Questions

To prevent no-shows and reserve your clinical suite, a 20% to 30% deposit is paid online during booking. The deposit is held safely in escrow and deducted from your total bill. You pay the remaining balance directly at the clinic after your treatment.

You can reschedule or cancel any appointment directly from your My Bookings dashboard up to 24 hours prior to your slot with zero penalty. Your deposit is instantly refunded or transferred to your new date.

Our clinical compliance board verifies medical licenses (MD Dermatology, CIDESCO esthetician certifications), US-FDA/CE device serial authorizations, and facility sterilization audits before approving any provider.
