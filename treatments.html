1. Hydro-Infusion & Extractions

2. Clinical Chemical Peels

Controlled acid application (TCA, Salicylic, Glycolic, Jessner) dissolved cellular cohesion in the stratum corneum to stimulate accelerated turnover.

Best For:
Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, sun damage, active acne, uneven skin tone.
  • Downtime: 2 to 4 days of micro-flaking
  • Cadence: 3-4 session series, 3 weeks apart
  • Pain Level: 2/5 (Mild tingling)
Explore Chemical Peels
Moderate Downtime (24-48h) 60-90 Mins

3. Microneedling & RF Tightening

Precision micro-channels combined with fractional radiofrequency energy deliver subdermal heat coagulation to trigger neocollagenesis and elastin formation.

Best For:
Atrophic acne scars, enlarged pores, skin laxity, fine lines, deep texture repair.
  • Downtime: 24-48h mild redness
  • Cadence: 3-6 sessions, 4-6 weeks apart
  • Pain Level: 1-2/5 (Topical numbing applied)
